# 📅 Day 06 – Underfitting, Overfitting, Bias-Variance Tradeoff & Loss Functions

**📆 Date:** 10 July 2026

---

## 📚 Topics Covered

### 1. Underfitting
- Model is too simple.
- Fails to capture the underlying patterns in the data.
- High training error.
- High testing error.

### 2. Overfitting
- Model learns both the actual patterns and the noise in the training data.
- Very low training error.
- High testing error.
- Poor generalization to unseen data.

### 3. Bias-Variance Tradeoff
- High Bias → Underfitting
- High Variance → Overfitting
- Goal: Build a model with low bias and low variance for better generalization.

### 4. Loss Function
A loss function measures how far the model's predictions are from the actual values.

Common Loss Functions:
- Mean Squared Error (MSE)
- Mean Absolute Error (MAE)
- Binary Cross Entropy
- Categorical Cross Entropy

- Understood how bias and variance affect model performance.
